# Research Completion Criteria

Apply to every mode. Record PASS, FAIL, or N/A with a reason and evidence; use N/A only when a
criterion is irrelevant to the requested output, never because it is difficult or retries expired.
These checks govern the strength of claims, not permission to deliver an honest partial result.

| Criterion | Completion evidence |
|---|---|
| Scope | The answer addresses the accepted question and decision criteria. |
| Provenance | Material factual claims trace to identifiable sources or observations. |
| Support | Evidence is sufficient for the exact claim; independence is assessed by origin and method, not worker count or source-bucket count. |
| Contradictions | Material disagreements are explained, resolved with evidence, or reflected as uncertainty in the conclusion. |
| Limitations | Known failures, access gaps, untested assumptions, and coverage limits are visible. |
| Currency | Time-sensitive claims are checked at the relevant date/version; older foundational evidence is not rejected merely for age. |
| Specificity | The deliverable is usable: concrete comparison criteria for decisions, scope/maturity for surveys, components and interfaces for feasibility. |
| Experiment (feasibility) | The highest-risk buildable assumption has a relevant reproducible result, or feasibility remains explicitly partial/untested. |

Search adjacent domains only when it can resolve a material gap; it is not a universal completion
requirement. Re-check affected claims after experiments or contradictory evidence. Failed evidence
criteria never become advisory after repeated attempts. Repair within the remaining entrypoint
budget or narrow the claim and conclude with the unresolved limitation; do not manufacture SUCCESS.
